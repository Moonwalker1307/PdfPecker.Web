    Limitations

    Changed in 1.8.0: Text and fonts, CSS, Images and SVG.

    PdfPecker is built for fast conversion of HTML templates you control, not for rendering arbitrary web pages. These are the known gaps; most are scope choices for speed and size, and they are the first candidates for future work. Anything not listed in What is supported should be assumed missing.

    By design

    • No JavaScript. Scripts are never run, so content must be in the HTML before conversion (render it server-side first).
    • No network access: http:/https: stylesheets and images are not downloaded. Supply them through your ResourceResolver, as files under BaseDirectory, or as data: URIs.
    • <canvas>, <iframe>, <video>, <audio>, <object> and <embed> are not drawn, nor are file, range, colour and image inputs. Form fields have no JavaScript (no calculations, formats or submit actions), and a field inside a transformed box, a running header or footer, or a position: fixed box is drawn but not fillable.

    Text and fonts Changed in 1.8.0

    • Without registered fonts or EmbedStandardFonts, text uses the standard PDF fonts, so characters are limited to the Windows-1252 set (Western European languages); other characters are replaced with ?. The bundled Liberation fonts add Central European, Greek and Cyrillic, but not CJK or other scripts: register fonts for those.
    • Complex-script shaping covers Arabic and Hebrew (and other scripts written with Arabic letters, such as Persian and Urdu). Devanagari and other Indic scripts, Thai, Khmer, Myanmar and Syriac are not shaped, so their vowel signs and conjuncts come out wrong, and Thai text breaks only at spaces. There is no vertical text (writing-mode).
    • In Latin and other simple scripts, shaping is limited to kerning, standard ligatures and features that swap one glyph for another (see OpenType features); contextual alternates and other features that depend on neighbouring characters are not applied, and small-caps is synthesized from scaled capitals.
    • Automatic hyphenation (hyphens: auto) has built-in patterns for English, Danish, German, Dutch and Norwegian only; other languages need their patterns in PdfOptions.Hyphenation. hyphenate-limit-chars is not supported.
    • SVG <text> uses only the first value of position lists and has no textPath; in a text chunk with right-to-left text, dx on its tspans is ignored.

    CSS Changed in 1.8.0

    • Rules inside @container blocks are skipped. A named page's :first is the document's first page, as in Chrome, not the first page of the named run. When @page :first, :left or :right margins give pages different content widths, the content is laid out at the narrowest width on every page; the first page is always a right page, also in right-to-left documents.
    • Selectors: a rule with an unsupported selector is skipped. Selectors inside :is(), :where() and :not() may have at most eight compound parts (one when nested in another of them).
    • filter: blur() turns the box into an image at 144 dpi, so text in it is no longer selectable. The colour filters keep everything as vectors, and drop-shadow() only adds the shadow as an image.
    • text-shadow blur is approximated with faint copies of the text rather than a true Gaussian blur, and decorations (underlines) get no shadow. text-overflow applies only at the end of the line. aspect-ratio does not take part in min-/max-content sizing (shrink-to-fit widths) and is ignored on tables. object-fit applies to raster images; an SVG file in <img> keeps its own preserveAspectRatio fit.
    • background-attachment: fixed scrolls with the box.

    Backgrounds and transparency

    • No background-clip: text; backgrounds of inline elements (<span>) show only their colour, not images or gradients.
    • Blurred box shadows and repeating gradients with translucent stops are blended onto the background colour rather than truly see-through; SVG gradient stops with transparency are blended onto white.

    Layout

    • Grid: no subgrid.
    • Multi-column: children are kept whole and balanced between columns; a single block is never split across columns.
    • Overflow: a positioned child with a z-index inside a clipping box stays stacked within that box.

    Images and SVG Changed in 1.8.0

    • No AVIF images: decoding them needs an AV1 video decoder, which is not planned. Convert them to WebP, PNG or JPEG.
    • SVG filters are applied in pixels at 144 dpi, so filtered shapes become an image (text keeps an invisible, selectable copy).

    Markdown and the PDF renderer

    • Markdown: no footnotes, definition lists, math or front matter.
    • PdfRenderer (PDF to image): no predefined CJK CMaps or non-embedded CJK fonts, and ICC profiles are not applied (ICC-based colours use their device equivalents). WebP output is lossless only (no lossy VP8), and JPEG output is baseline only (no progressive or arithmetic coding).
