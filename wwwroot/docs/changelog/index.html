    Changelog

    What changed in each PdfPecker release on NuGet, newest first. The manual for any of these versions can be downloaded as a PDF with the version picker next to Download PDF; the source of every change is in the PdfPecker repository.

    1.16.0

    Released 20 October 2026.

    • Borders on inline elements: badges, keyboard keys and dotted underlines drawn with border on a <span> are painted, with box-decoration-break deciding how a border split over several lines is drawn.
    • SVG dashed strokes with stroke-dasharray, stroke-dashoffset and pathLength, and text along a path with textPath.
    • Grid rows break across pages where they start instead of moving whole and leaving a gap, and a margin that runs past the end of a page is dropped, as in Chrome. A box's top and bottom borders stay on the page with its content, and a paragraph that ends just past the page keeps its widows.
    • A <fieldset>'s legend sits on its top border, and groove and ridge borders have Chrome's two shades.
    • Form controls are sized as in Chrome: text fields in tables, textarea rows, drop-downs, date fields and check boxes, and controls the page gives its own border have Chrome's plain square look.
    • Text can break before and after inline-blocks, images and form controls, so a label no longer wraps a word down next to its input.
    • Line heights are rounded to Chrome's layout units, and line breaking counts the kerning next to spaces, so lines break where Chrome breaks them more often.
    • Text extraction reads the standard Symbol and ZapfDingbats fonts (αβγ, ✓✔) instead of returning their raw codes.
    • Bounded layout time for deeply nested flex, grid, tables, columns and keep-with-next chains found by fuzzing.
    • Line positions, page breaks, forms, fieldsets and SVG dashes look different from 1.15.0; most documents move by a pixel or less.

    1.15.0

    Released 19 October 2026.

    • Invoice XML from invoice data: InvoiceData writes Factur-X / ZUGFeRD EN 16931 and XRechnung CII, and Peppol BIS 3.0 and XRechnung UBL, with the totals and VAT breakdown calculated and the EN 16931 rules checked.
    • A ready-made invoice template: InvoiceTemplate.ToPdf renders an A4 invoice with your logo and colour in English, German or Danish, with an EPC payment QR code and the Factur-X XML embedded.
    • Compacting: PdfCompactor makes any PDF smaller, losslessly by default, or with images downsampled and recompressed as JPEG.
    • A cell's rowspan stops at the end of its table header, body or footer, as in HTML, instead of throwing NullReferenceException.
    • Deeply nested break-inside: avoid boxes in columns are laid out in bounded time; the output is unchanged.
    • PdfSignatureVerifier reports a signature whose signer key cannot be read instead of throwing OverflowException (Windows, .NET 8 and 9).
    • No other output changes: PDFs from 1.14.2 templates are byte-identical.

    1.14.2

    Released 18 October 2026.

    • A table whose first row does not fit on the rest of a page starts on the next page, instead of leaving its header row behind at the bottom of the page. Chrome does the same.
    • In tables with automatic layout, columns with a width keep it next to columns of long text, as in Chrome. Tables without column widths are laid out as before.

    1.14.1

    Released 18 October 2026.

    • Collapsed table borders stay visible over cell backgrounds: shaded header and footer rows keep their vertical lines, and striped rows keep the line above them, as in Chrome. Tables with border-collapse and cell backgrounds look different from 1.14.0.

    1.14.0

    Released 18 October 2026.

    • Backgrounds on inline elements: <span>, <mark>, <code> and badges get images, gradients, border-radius and clipping to text, not just a colour, and box-decoration-break decides how a background split over several lines is drawn.
    • Markdown footnotes, definition lists and front matter, with footnotes at the bottom of the page in a PDF.
    • hyphenate-limit-chars for the shortest hyphenated word and the fewest letters around a hyphen.
    • Rows of collapsed tables with lines only under or over each row now have Chrome's height, and balanced columns keep break-inside: avoid boxes whole without growing taller than needed.
    • Bounded work for repeating gradients and deeply nested decorated inline elements on hostile input. Tables with collapsed row lines, rounded inline badges and some multi-column layouts look different from 1.13.0.

    1.13.0

    Released 18 October 2026.

    • Barcodes: QR codes (versions 1 to 40, error correction L to H), Code 128 and EAN-13 as vector SVG for your templates, and Barcode.ToDataUri for an <img>, a CSS background or a Markdown image.
    • EPC payment QR codes (GiroCode) for euro transfers, with the IBAN, BIC, amount and field lengths checked.
    • Images to PDF: PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP and TIFF images one per page, at their own size or fitted to a page size with margins.
    • HTML to images in one call with HtmlToImage.
    • No output changes: PDFs from 1.12.0 templates are byte-identical.

    1.12.0

    Released 18 October 2026.

    • Indic, Thai and Lao shaping: Devanagari, Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam, Thai and Lao are shaped as HarfBuzz shapes them, and combining marks in Latin, Greek and Cyrillic are placed on their letters.
    • background-clip: text (also as -webkit-background-clip).
    • Tables across pages: <tfoot> repeats at the bottom of every page, and tables inside flex and grid items break across pages instead of moving whole.
    • Reproducible output: with CreationDate or SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH the same input gives a byte-identical PDF, also for merging, page tools, stamping and form filling.
    • Cancellable signing, AddValidationDataAsync, and cancellation-token and Stream overloads across the API.
    • PdfPeckerException as the base of the library's own exceptions, and file output that leaves an existing file untouched when a conversion fails.
    • StampHtml ignores the footer, header, watermark and other document-level options.
    • text-transform: uppercase turns ß into SS and handles Turkish dotted and dotless i; hyphens: auto hyphenates decomposed words; CSS integers are read correctly on servers with an Arabic or Persian culture.
    • Bounded work on malformed JPEG 2000, JBIG2, e-invoices, attachments and CSS, and less memory for JPEG and WebP output. Tables with a <tfoot>, tables in flex and grid, and Indic and Thai text look different from 1.11.0 on purpose.

    1.11.0

    Released 18 October 2026.

    • Subgrid (CSS Grid Level 2): grid-template-columns and grid-template-rows: subgrid line nested grids up with their parent's tracks.
    • Multi-column layout splits paragraphs, lists and tables across columns and onto later pages, as Chrome does, instead of keeping each child whole in the first columns.
    • Percentage heights resolve against definite containing blocks, as in browsers.
    • Blurred box shadows, translucent gradient stops and SVG stop-opacity are truly transparent, blending with what is behind them instead of with white.
    • Hyphenation in Swedish, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Polish; French and Italian also hyphenate words with elisions such as l'ordinateur.
    • Correct word widths in paragraphs with an inline-block, and shaping of Arabic Extended-C characters outside the Basic Multilingual Plane.
    • Bounded work on crafted fonts and malformed PDFs, and linear time for Markdown autolinks, nested inline-blocks and headings kept with nested lists, flex rows and tables. No public API changes.

    1.10.0

    Released 18 October 2026.

    • PDF to TIFF: a whole PDF as one multi-page TIFF, or single pages, in colour, greyscale or black and white with CCITT Group 4, LZW, Deflate or PackBits compression.
    • Read the invoice back from a received Factur-X, ZUGFeRD or XRechnung PDF with PdfEInvoice.Read.
    • Trusted timestamps: a timestamp's time is used to check the signer's certificate only when its authority is trusted (TimestampTrusted), so an untrusted timestamp no longer makes an expired certificate pass.
    • PdfLtvOptions.UrlFilter decides which OCSP and CRL addresses long-term validation may contact, and fetching is bounded in URLs, redirects and time.
    • ::first-line styles on small capitals and mixed-direction text no longer lose their style or throw, and Arabic text in PDF/A-2u, 3u and PDF/UA maps every glyph back to Unicode.
    • Tighter limits for hostile input found by fuzzing (images, SVG filters, soft masks, fonts, signature stores), more renderer features (soft-mask Matte, CalRGB and CalGray), and symbol packages with SourceLink on NuGet.

    1.9.0

    Released 16 October 2026.

    • Chinese, Japanese and Korean line breaking with kinsoku rules, CSS line-break and word-break: keep-all.
    • External signers: sign with a key in an HSM, smart card or cloud key vault, and PdfSigner.SignAsync.
    • Signature verification and long-term validation (PAdES B-LT and B-LTA).
    • Text extraction from any PDF, with the position of every line and word.
    • Fill and flatten the forms of existing PDFs.
    • Container queries and the cq units.
    • Footnotes with float: footnote, numbered calls and a styled footnote area.
    • Each page laid out at its own width when :first, :left or :right change it, and right-to-left page order.
    • Blurred text shadows with a true Gaussian blur, shadows on underlines, and selectable text under filter: blur().
    • aspect-ratio in shrink-to-fit widths, with min-/max-height and on tables; object-fit for SVG images; and complex selectors inside :is(), :where(), :not() and :has().

    1.8.0

    Released 16 October 2026.

    • Variable fonts: weight, width, italic and optical size axes, font-variation-settings, kerning and CFF2 outlines (see Fonts).
    • OpenType figures and features: font-variant-numeric (tabular, old-style, fractions and more) and font-feature-settings (see Fonts).
    • border-image, CSS filter, clip-path on HTML elements, blend modes, 3D border styles, inset box shadows, ::first-letter and ::first-line, @counter-style and relative colours.
    • Every SVG filter primitive, with primitive subregions, and text inside filtered SVG stays selectable.
    • <image> in SVG, SVG watermarks, and BMP and TIFF images.
    • @import, and :first, :left and :right page margins and sizes.
    • The manual moved from the README to pdfpecker.net/docs, and the NuGet package links to it and has an icon.

    1.7.0

    Released 15 October 2026.

    • Stamping: put a page of one PDF over or under the pages of another.
    • Attachments: attach any file to a PDF and read attachments back.
    • Automatic hyphenation with hyphens: auto.
    • Add, change or remove passwords on existing PDFs.
    • Extract, reorder, remove, rotate and split pages.
    • JPEG and WebP output from PdfToImage.
    • PDF/A-2u, 3u, 4 and 4f, and PDF/UA-2.
    • Right-to-left text in SVG, and right-to-left ellipsis.

    1.6.0

    Released 15 October 2026.

    • Right-to-left text with Arabic and Hebrew shaping.
    • Named pages, running headers and footers from HTML, table of contents page numbers with target-counter() and leader(), and conic-gradient() (see Paged media).
    • Much more of CSS3: modern colour syntax, calc() and the other math functions, @layer, nesting, @supports, @property, media queries, :has() and margin boxes.
    • Sign into an existing empty signature field, merging keeps tagging and PDF/UA, and PdfOptions.MissingCharacter reports characters no font has.

    1.5.1

    Released 14 October 2026.

    • Targets .NET 8 and 9 as well as .NET 10.
    • Complete third-party licence notices, used as the NuGet package licence. 1.5.0 was never published, because nuget.org did not accept its licence expression.

    1.4.0

    Released 14 October 2026.

    • Fillable PDF forms from HTML inputs, check boxes, radio buttons, selects and text areas.

    1.3.0

    Released 14 October 2026.

    • Tagged PDF and PDF/UA-1 output.
    • SVG masks, patterns, markers and filters, clipping in table cells, and double rounded borders.

    1.2.0

    Released 14 October 2026.

    • Attach PDFs to Peppol and OIOUBL (UBL) e-invoices.
    • Faster HTML to PDF, with half the memory allocations and decoded images reused.

    1.1.1

    Released 13 October 2026.

    • The README shows its logo on nuget.org and has a NuGet badge. No changes to the library.

    1.1.0

    Released 13 October 2026.

    • Factur-X and ZUGFeRD e-invoices.
    • SVG, GIF and WebP images.
    • Streaming PDF merging: stream and async overloads with one input in memory at a time.
    • calc(), min(), max() and clamp(), :nth-of-type, :is(), :where(), object-fit, text-overflow, outline, text-shadow and aspect-ratio.
    • Header and footer heights are rounded to whole points, as Chrome does.

    1.0.0

    Released 12 October 2026.

    • The first release: HTML and Markdown to PDF and PDF to PNG with its own HTML parser, CSS engine, layout engine, PDF writer and renderer, and no dependencies outside the .NET base class library.
